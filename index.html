🩹 Offline Health Guide

Skin irritation after flood exposure

Wash exposed skin with clean water and soap, keep it clean and dry, and seek medical care if symptoms worsen.

Safe drinking water

Follow local health authority guidance for boiling, disinfection, or other approved purification methods.

Minor cuts and wounds

Clean with safe water and cover with a clean dressing. Seek care for deep wounds, severe bleeding, or infection signs.
